The Dead Sea

Introduction

The Dead Sea is one of Jordan's most famous natural attractions. It is located in the Jordan Rift Valley and lies more than 400 meters below sea level, making it the lowest land-based point on Earth's surface.

Salt and Minerals

The Dead Sea receives water from several streams, including the Jordan River, but it has no natural outlet. Because water leaves mainly through evaporation, the remaining water contains a very high concentration of salts and minerals.

History

The area around the Dead Sea has a long history. People have lived, hunted, and farmed in the Jordan Valley since the Stone Age. More than 200 archaeological sites have also been discovered in the wider area.

Tourism

Today, the Dead Sea is an important tourist destination in Jordan. Visitors come to enjoy its unique landscape and experience its highly salty water.
