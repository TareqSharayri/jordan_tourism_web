Petra

Introduction

Petra is one of the most famous archaeological sites in Jordan and one of the world's most remarkable ancient cities. It is located in southern Jordan and was the capital of the Nabataean Kingdom.

History

Petra became an important trading center because it was located at a crossroads connecting Arabia, Egypt, and Syria-Phoenicia.

Architecture

The city is famous for its buildings and monuments carved directly into the red sandstone mountains. One of its most famous monuments is Al-Khazneh, also known as The Treasury.

Water Management

The Nabataeans developed an advanced water management system that allowed them to collect and store rainwater in the dry environment surrounding Petra.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Petra was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1985. Today, it is an important tourist destination and a symbol of Jordan's rich history and cultural heritage.
